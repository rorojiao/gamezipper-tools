How to use this SWOT generator

  1. Type the subject of your analysis in the top field (e.g. "our SaaS analytics platform").
  2. Click a prompt suggestion chip to insert it into the matching quadrant — then customize the wording.
  3. Fill all four quadrants with 3-7 short bullet points. Strengths/Weaknesses are internal, Opportunities/Threats are external.
  4. Watch the live preview update as you type, and the counter chips at the top show how complete each quadrant is.
  5. Export to Markdown for Notion / Confluence / GitHub, plain text for email, or print to PDF via your browser.
